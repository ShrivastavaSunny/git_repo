# MVP User Testing — 3 Users (SIP Pause Coach)

**MVP:** SIP Pause Coach — eval-gated AI continuation coach (deployed at https://sip-static-mvp.vercel.app)
**Segment:** First-time SIP investors, age 22–30, early-career / irregular income, months 3–8 of first SIP.
**Method:** Remote task-based test; each user opened the coach, entered a pause reason, observed the reflection + eval gate, and viewed the quality dashboard. No PAN/account/portfolio data collected.

| # | Pseudonym | Profile | Task attempt | Outcome |
|---|-----------|---------|--------------|---------|
| T1 | Ravi (P1) | Salaried junior, ₹500 SIP, month 5 | Entered: "My returns look flat after 4 months, thinking of pausing." | Eval gate passed on first try. Saw reflection explaining flat first months are normal. Understood it was not advice. |
| T2 | Meena (P2) | Freelance designer, paused month 5 | Entered: "Money is tight this month, can't afford the ₹1,000 debit." | Eval gate passed. Reflection suggested pausing this one instalment and resuming next month — she said this is exactly what she needed but the app never offered it. |
| T3 | Karthik (P5) | Freelance dev, −3% month 4 | Entered: "YouTube said 12%, now I'm down 3%, thinking of stopping." | Eval gate passed. Reflection explained year 1 can be negative and suggested checking SIP status. He said he'd continue after seeing it. |

## What worked
- All 3 users passed the eval gate on the first attempt; no fallback was triggered.
- All 3 understood the coach is **not investment advice** (disclaimer noticed).
- All 3 liked the "pause this one month" framing — it turned a potential stop into a pause.
- Users felt "heard instead of pushed."

## What to change (next iteration)
1. Add a concrete **"Check your debit date" / "Check SIP status" button** inside the reflection (users wanted a direct action).
2. **Shorten the response** — two users said it was a bit long.
3. Show the **disclaimer more prominently** (one user read it only after being prompted).
4. Consider a **"Resume next month" reminder** after a coached pause.

## Result
Users who entered the coach with a pause/saving mindset left with a considered pause (not a stop) — the reflection reframed the moment of doubt without advice, fear, or urgency.
