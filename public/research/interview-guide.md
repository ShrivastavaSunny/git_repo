# Urban Company repeat-booking interview guide

**Purpose:** Understand why people complete a later home-service booking within 90 days or do not, even when nothing went wrong.

## Eligibility
- Completed an Urban Company core-category booking (AC, cleaning, plumbing, salon, pest control or carpenter) in the last 6 months.
- Exactly 3 to 4 returners and 2 to 3 non-returners in a 6-person study.
- More than one core category across the sample.
- Record total completed booking count, household role and category for context.

Do not request screenshots, invoices, addresses, phone numbers, account details or personal documents.

## Consent
Explain purpose, voluntary participation, recording choice and withdrawal. Use a short consent statement before the interview. Store only anonymized notes.

## 30-minute interview

1. **Anchor booking** (3 min)
   - “Please describe the most recent core-category booking.”
   - “How did you decide the service was needed?”
   - “How did you find Urban Company?”

2. **Completion moment** (5 min)
   - “What happened immediately after the professional left?”
   - “What, if anything, did you expect to happen next?”
   - “What did you do with the invoice or follow-up message?”

3. **Next 90 days** (8 min)
   - “What other household or personal-care services did you need?”
   - “For each need, what did you do: ignore it, handle it yourself, ask family/neighbours, use WhatsApp, call a known provider or use an app?”

4. **Return or non-return** (8 min)
   - “What made you book again within 90 days?” or “What stopped you?”
   - “Was price, quality, timing or memory the main factor? What would have changed your decision?”

5. **Home-service mental model** (4 min)
   - “Do you plan these, handle them reactively or avoid thinking about them?”
   - “What would make planning feel useful rather than pushy?”

## Analysis template
For each respondent record:

- Anonymous ID
- Returner/non-returner
- Anchor category
- Other categories mentioned
- Total booking count
- Decision trigger
- How found Urban Company
- Post-service action
- First next household need
- Alternative channel/workaround
- Return trigger or stopping cause
- Planned/reactive/avoidant orientation
- Evidence quote, with personal details removed
