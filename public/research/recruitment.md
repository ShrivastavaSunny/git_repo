# Target-user recruitment brief

## Segment
Adults who manage household or personal-care decisions and completed a core Urban Company booking within the last 6 months.

## Quota
- 6 people total
- 3–4 people who completed another booking within 90 days
- 2–3 people who did not
- Cover AC, deep cleaning, plumbing, salon and/or pest control
- Include at least two households with different household types

## Do not collect
- Booking screenshots
- Invoices
- Addresses
- Phone numbers
- Account information
- Personal documents

## Screener
1. Did you complete a core-category Urban Company booking in the last 6 months?
2. What category was it?
3. Have you personally made any household or personal-care service bookings during the last 90 days?
4. How many Urban Company bookings have you completed in total, approximately?
5. What household role do you have in deciding or arranging home services?

## Incentive
Use a fair, category-neutral participation thank-you only if permitted by the host program. Do not offer UC discounts or coupons.
