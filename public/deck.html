01 · METRIC DECOMPOSITION

The metric is a chain of visible and less visible customer outcomes

1 · Need recurs

Seasonal, symptom-led or personal-care need exists in 90 days

Influence: low

Measure: category + customer profile survey; public benchmark

2 · Customer remembers

Need becomes actionable while the app is out of mind

Influence: high

Measure: reminder awareness and “why now” response

3 · Customer acts

Chooses provider and completes a service

Influence: medium

Measure only with consented first-party booking data

Public/customer-measured proxy: 90-day plan activation → revisit intent → service-category selection without nudging unneeded work.

04 · RESEARCH PLAN & TARGET SEGMENT

Target: 25–45-year-old household decision-makers with a completed core-service booking

Recruitment

  • 6 respondents
  • At least 3 returners / 3 non-returners within 90 days
  • More than one core category
  • Record total bookings only; no documents or addresses

Interview method

30-minute semi-structured incident interview: decision → visit → post-service → next household need → workaround → trigger or stop.

Ask: “How did you decide the job was needed?” “What did you do next?”

Build status: the research protocol is ready, but no real interview respondents were available to this build. This slide intentionally does not claim findings that were not collected.

05 · PROBLEM DEFINITION

Customers lose the link between today’s completed service and tomorrow’s household need

Segment
Nuclear/shared households or senior households acting as household manager

Scenario
Immediately after a completed core booking and across the next 90 days

Intended outcome
Customer recognizes and acts on an existing need sooner

Root cause hypothesis
Home-care needs are intermittent and event-led; the app has no customer-owned memory/planning surface.

Workarounds
Calendar notes, WhatsApp lists, family prompts, local providers, repeated app search.

User value: less mental load. Business value: creates more relevant re-entry moments without changing price, professional earnings or service operations.

06 · MVP

Home Care Plan lets customers create a 90-day household view

INPUT

Household type + need areas + latest visit date

OUTPUT

One visible suggested planning window per selected need

GUARDRAILS

  • No professional or UC messaging
  • No scrape or external account access
  • No urgency claims or discount language
Live prototype: Try Home Care Plan

The MVP uses customer-supplied choices and public/general intervals only. It requires no personal documents or location.

09 · RISKS & MITIGATION

The biggest risk is planning tasks the customer does not need

Unneeded nudges

Default intervals become recommendations

Mitigate: user-choice-only, explicit planning language, symptom-based options, easy delete

False confidence

Dates look definitive

Mitigate: suggested windows, disclaimers, no diagnosis or safety claim

Privacy

Household preferences become sensitive

Mitigate: local-only browser state, no account identity, no documents

Bias / poor evidence

Negative app reviews dominate

Mitigate: public-source labels, 6-person mixed follow-up, golden-set audit
